About Vijram Tools Co

With over 10 years of experience in the power tools spares industry, Quality Tools has built a reputation for reliability, quality, and trust. We specialize in supplying durable armatures, stators, switches, bearings, and connectors — serving a wide range of models across angle grinders, marble cutters, rotary hammers, demolition hammers, drills, cutoff machines, and paint mixers.

We proudly deal with top global brands such as Bosch, HiKOKI, Dewalt, DCA, and Dongcheng, while also offering suitable generic spares that provide cost‑effective alternatives without compromising on performance. This ensures that workshops, contractors, and retailers can source both branded and compatible parts to meet their operational needs.

Our mission is simple: to deliver performance, precision, and trust — backed by a decade of proven experience in the power tools spares market.
